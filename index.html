Flower Care Instructions — Authentic Flowers

Add flower food to clean water. Change water every 2–3 days with fresh, cold water. Keep flowers at room temperature and away from direct sunlight. Trim stems diagonally about ½ inch each time the water is changed.

Flower Care Instructions — Eternal Flowers

Eternal flowers require little to no maintenance. Keep them in a safe area and avoid excessive handling to maintain their shape and appearance. With proper care, they are designed to be a long-lasting keepsake.

Order Care & Responsibility
