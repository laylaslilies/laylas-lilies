FLOWER CARE INSTRUCTIONS — Authentic Flowers

To keep fresh flowers looking their best:
• Add flower food to clean water.
• Change water every 2-3 days with fresh, cold water.
• Keep flowers at room temperature and away from direct sunlight.
• Trim stems diagonally about ½ inch each time the water is changed.

FLOWER CARE INSTRUCTIONS — Eternal Flowers

• Eternal flowers require little to no maintenance.
• Keep them in a safe area and avoid excessive handling to maintain their shape and appearance.
• With proper care, they are designed to be a long-lasting keepsake.
